# BM25

Classic keyword search: scores a chunk by how often the question's words appear in it, weighted by how rare those words are.

- `k1` (1.5): how fast repeated words stop adding score
- `b` (0.75): how much long chunks are penalised
- ✅ Great for exact terms (names, codes)
- ❌ Can't match across languages: a Malay word never appears in Korean text
