# Retrieval metrics

- **Recall@k**: share of questions with at least one correct page in the top k
- **MRR@10**: average of 1/rank of the first correct hit (1.0 = always first; rank 2 = 0.5)
- **NDCG@10**: rewards finding *more* correct pages, nearer the top

Always report per language: en / ms / ko / mixed.
